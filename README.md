## 🔒 Data Protection & Information Compliance
As part of this week's governance modules, I examined critical UK legislation governing data handling and privacy[cite: 5]:
* **Data Protection Act (DPA):** Governs how organizations collect, store, and use personal information securely[cite: 5].
* **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):** Protects individual privacy rights, ensures processing transparency, and minimizes breach risks.
* **Freedom of Information Act (FOIA):** Grants public rights to request information from public authorities to maintain organizational accountability.
* **Environmental Information Regulations (EIR):** Ensures transparent access to environmental data held by public bodies.
